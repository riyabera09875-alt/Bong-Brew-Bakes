OUR STORY

It started with one tiny
shop in Gariahat.

Three years ago, we opened with a filter coffee machine and a stubborn idea: that nolen gur belongs next to espresso, and kosha mangsho belongs in a sandwich. Two outlets and a cloud kitchen later, we still do things slowly.
